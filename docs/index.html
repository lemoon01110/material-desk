Material Desk

A calm new tab for Chrome: a cookie clock, search, weather, to-dos and bookmarks, colored from a wallpaper you choose. Free, open source, and what you save stays on your device.

Chrome Web Store (coming soon) Source on GitHub Privacy policy

Cookie clock

A round Material 3 clock and date, in the style of the End4 desktop.

Search

A long search bar that uses the search engine set in Chrome, or one of six engines you pick, with suggestions for Google and DuckDuckGo.

Weather

Now, the next hours and 10 days, plus air quality, from Open-Meteo.

To-dos

Lists, due dates, priorities, tags, subtasks and repeating tasks.

Bookmarks

Browse, search and edit your Chrome bookmarks in a grid or list.

Your colors

Material You colors from a built-in wallpaper or your own picture.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no ads. Your settings, to-dos and wallpaper stay in your browser. Only the weather and search suggestion services get what they need to answer. Read the privacy policy.
