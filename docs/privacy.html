Material Desk

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026. Applies to the Material Desk extension for Google Chrome.

In short

  • Material Desk has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • The developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data. There is no Material Desk server.
  • Your settings, to-dos and wallpaper are stored only in your browser on your device.
  • To show the weather and search suggestions, the page contacts a few outside services directly from your browser. Each one gets only what it needs for that feature, listed below.
  • Your data is never sold, and it is not used for advertising or to decide creditworthiness or lending.

What is stored on your device

Material Desk keeps the following in Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage.local), the page's local storage and IndexedDB. All of it stays in your Chrome profile on this computer.

  • Settings: temperature units, whether to use your location, the city you typed, search engine, search suggestions on or off, which parts of the page are shown, column sizes and panel opacity.
  • To-dos: your lists, sections, to-do titles, notes, due dates and times, priorities, tags, subtasks and repeat rules.
  • Weather cache: the last forecast and the place name it was for, so a new tab shows the weather at once.
  • Wallpaper and colors: the built-in wallpaper you picked or the image you uploaded (kept in IndexedDB, never uploaded anywhere), and the colors made from it.

Bookmarks are not copied by Material Desk. The bookmarks column reads and changes Chrome's own bookmarks through Chrome's bookmarks API when you browse, add, edit, move or delete them. If you use Chrome sync, Chrome syncs your bookmarks as it always does. Site icons next to bookmarks come from Chrome's own icon cache on your device. Material Desk does not ask any web service for them.

Which outside services are contacted

These requests go straight from your browser to the service over HTTPS. Like any web request, each one also shows the service your IP address and basic browser information. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

ServiceWhenWhat it receives
Open-Meteo (forecast and air quality) When the weather card loads or refreshes Latitude and longitude, rounded to 3 decimals (about 100 m)
Open-Meteo (geocoding) When you use a typed city instead of your location The city name you typed
BigDataCloud When your location is used, to show the city name Latitude and longitude
ipinfo.io Only as a fallback, when no location or city is available, or the city name lookup fails Nothing besides the request itself. It uses the request's IP address to estimate your city.
Google search suggestions While you type in the search bar, if suggestions are on and Google (or an engine other than DuckDuckGo) is chosen The text typed so far
DuckDuckGo search suggestions While you type in the search bar, if suggestions are on and DuckDuckGo is chosen The text typed so far

When you run a search, open a bookmark or click a Google apps link, Chrome simply goes to that website, the same as typing the address yourself. That website's own privacy policy applies.

Your location

Location is used only for the weather card. When "Use my location" is on, the page asks Chrome for your device location and sends the rounded coordinates to Open-Meteo and BigDataCloud as listed above. Material Desk does not keep a history of your locations. You can turn "Use my location" off in settings and type a city instead, or hide the weather card completely.

What Material Desk does not do

  • It does not send anything to the developer or to any analytics, advertising or tracking service.
  • It does not read your browsing history or the content of the websites you visit. It has no content scripts.
  • It does not load or run code from the internet. All of its code is in the extension package.
  • It does not sell, rent or share your data, and does not use it for any purpose other than the features described here.

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Stop location use: settings, turn off "Use my location" and type a city.
  • Stop search suggestions: settings, turn off search suggestions. Nothing you type is then sent until you run a search.
  • Hide the weather: settings, turn off the weather card. No weather or location requests are then made.
  • Export your data: settings, "Export data" saves your settings and to-dos to a file on your computer.
  • Reset settings: settings, "Reset settings" puts every setting back to its default. It keeps your to-dos and bookmarks.
  • Delete to-dos: delete them in the to-do column.
  • Delete everything: remove the extension (chrome://extensions, then Remove). Chrome then deletes all data Material Desk stored, including to-dos, settings, the weather cache and your uploaded wallpaper. Your Chrome bookmarks are Chrome's own and stay.

Permissions

  • New tab override: shows Material Desk when you open a new tab.
  • Storage: keeps your settings and to-dos on your device.
  • Bookmarks: shows and edits your Chrome bookmarks in the bookmarks column.
  • Favicon: shows site icons from Chrome's icon cache next to bookmarks.
  • Geolocation: gets your location for the weather card when "Use my location" is on.
  • Search: sends searches to Chrome's search engine settings.
  • Host access to the weather, geocoding and suggestion services listed above, and to no other websites.

Children

Material Desk is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If Material Desk ever changes what it stores or sends, this page will be updated with a new effective date before that version is released, and the change will be described in the release notes. The history of this page is public in the GitHub repository.
